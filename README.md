# Dashboard Formula Documentation

This document explains the business formulas used in the Deal Room dashboard.

---

## 1. Total Opportunities

### Purpose
Shows the total number of opportunities available in the sales pipeline.

### Formula

Total Opportunities = COUNT(Opportunity ID)

### Example

160 opportunity records

Result = 160

---

## 2. Weighted Forecast

### Purpose

Estimates the expected revenue by multiplying each opportunity's value with its probability of closing successfully.

### Formula

Weighted Forecast = Forecast Value × Probability

### Example

Forecast Value = $100,000

Probability = 70%

Weighted Forecast = $70,000

---

## 3. Conversion Rate

### Purpose

Measures how many opportunities successfully move from one pipeline stage to the next.

### Formula

Conversion Rate =
(Opportunities moved to next stage /
Total opportunities in current stage) × 100

### Example

Qualified Opportunities = 100

Moved to Discovery = 75

Conversion Rate = 75%

---

## 4. Stage Ageing

### Purpose

Calculates how long an opportunity has remained in its current pipeline stage.

### Formula

Stage Age =
Today's Date − Stage Entered Date

### Example

Stage Entered Date = 1 July

Today's Date = 10 July

Stage Age = 9 days

---

## 5. Stalled Deal

### Purpose

Identifies opportunities that have remained inactive beyond the configured threshold.

### Formula

If Stage Age > Stage Threshold

Stalled = TRUE

Otherwise

Stalled = FALSE

### Example

POC Threshold = 21 days

Current Stage Age = 28 days

Result = Stalled

---

## 6. Active POCs

### Purpose

Counts all opportunities currently in the POC stage.

### Formula

Active POCs =
COUNT(Opportunities WHERE Pipeline Stage = 'POC / Technical Evaluation')

### Example

Opportunity 1 = POC

Opportunity 2 = Proposal

Opportunity 3 = POC

Result = 2 Active POCs

---

## 7. Win-Loss Ratio

### Purpose

Compares the number of successful deals against unsuccessful deals.

### Formula

Win-Loss Ratio =
Closed Won ÷ Closed Lost

### Example

Closed Won = 40

Closed Lost = 20

Result = 2 : 1

---

## 8. Partner Contribution

### Purpose

Measures the business generated by each OEM partner.

### Formula

Partner Contribution (%) =
(Partner Revenue ÷ Total Revenue) × 100

### Example

Cisco Revenue = $500,000

Total Revenue = $2,000,000

Partner Contribution = 25%
